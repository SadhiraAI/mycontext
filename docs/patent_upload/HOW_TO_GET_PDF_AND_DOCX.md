## Get a PDF (for direct upload)

1. Open **Provisional_Patent_Specification_FULL_Dhiraj_Pokhrel.html** in your browser (Chrome or Edge).  
   **Important:** Open it from the `patent_upload` folder (e.g. double-click the file or use File → Open) so that the embedded figures (in `../patent_figures/`) load. The PDF will then include all 5 figures.
2. Press **Ctrl+P** (Print).
3. Set **Destination** to **Save as PDF** (or “Microsoft Print to PDF”).
4. Click **Save** and name it: `Provisional_Patent_Specification_Dhiraj_Pokhrel.pdf`.
5. Upload this PDF in Patent Center as the **Specification** document.

**Cover sheet PDF (if needed):**

- Open **Cover_Sheet_PTO_SB16_Dhiraj_Pokhrel.html** in the browser.
- Print → Save as PDF → save as `Cover_Sheet_Dhiraj_Pokhrel.pdf`.

---

## Get a DOCX (for direct upload)

1. Open **Microsoft Word**.
2. Go to **File → Open** and select **Provisional_Patent_Specification_FULL_Dhiraj_Pokhrel.html** (you may need to choose “All Files” in the file type dropdown).
3. Word will open the HTML. Go to **File → Save As**.
4. Choose **Word Document (*.docx)** and save as: `Provisional_Patent_Specification_Dhiraj_Pokhrel.docx`.
5. Upload this DOCX in Patent Center as the **Specification** document.

---

## What to upload in Patent Center

1. **Cover sheet:** Use Patent Center’s **Web ADS** (recommended) **or** upload the cover sheet PDF from above.
2. **Specification:** Upload either:
   - `Provisional_Patent_Specification_Dhiraj_Pokhrel.pdf`, or  
   - `Provisional_Patent_Specification_Dhiraj_Pokhrel.docx`  
   (from the steps above).
3. **Drawings:** Upload your `Patent_Figures.pdf` (or the 5 PNGs from the `patent_figures` folder).

---

## Before you submit

- Add your **signature** and **date** on the declaration (you can do this in Word before saving as DOCX, or print the PDF, sign, scan, and re-upload if the system allows).
- In Patent Center, fill in **email** and **phone** where requested (they are not in the HTML to protect privacy).

You can now upload the PDF or DOCX directly to Patent Center.
